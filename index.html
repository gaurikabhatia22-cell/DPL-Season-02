Happy 2nd Anniversary,
my love. 🤍

I made all of this for you because I wanted to find some way to show you just how much you mean to me. And honestly, I don't think any website, any gift, or even all the words in the world could ever properly explain how much I love you.

We've known each other for eight years, which still feels crazy to me sometimes. We were literally just two kids when our story started, and somehow, after everything that happened between then and now, we found our way back to each other.

But these last two years have been different.

These two years of being together, talking about absolutely everything, telling each other about our days, laughing at the stupidest things, fighting, making up, supporting each other and just being us have taught me what it actually means to love someone.

You've taught me patience. You've taught me kindness. You've taught me how important it is to support someone and, most importantly, how to never give up on the person you love.

And I think that's one of the things I love most about you.

No matter how difficult things get, you've never been someone who simply gives up on me. You've always found a way to understand me, stand beside me and remind me that you believe in me.

Our story started eight years ago, but somewhere along the way, you became so much more than just a person I love. You became the person I want to tell everything to, the person I want beside me when something amazing happens, and the person I naturally imagine when I think about my future.

And if there's one thing I'm completely sure about, it's that I want there to be a lot more seasons of us.

So, Dev—

Thank you.

Thank you for loving me the way you do.
Thank you for putting up with me. 😭
Thank you for supporting me even when I get annoyed by how much you support me.
Thank you for all the poems, all the calls, all the “I love yous” in languages I didn't even know existed, and all the little things you probably don't realise I notice.

Thank you for being you.

Our story has never been completely perfect, but somehow, it's always been ours. And I wouldn't trade that for anything.

So here's to the little fourth-grade kids who had no idea what was coming.

Here's to Bus 7.

Here's to August 2024.

Here's to 02.09.2024.

And here's to Season 02.

Because if these last two years have taught me anything, it's that I don't want this to be the end of our story.

I want all the seasons after this one too. 🤍
